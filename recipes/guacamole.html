Description

A homemade guacamole recipe is quick and easy, and the results are delicious and healthy—and so much better than store-bought options. All you need are ripe avocados, onion, tomatoes, cilantro, jalapeño, lime juice, garlic, and salt. You can skip the tomato and/or the onion if you prefer, and use just as much jalapeño or another chile as you like.

Ingredients

  • Avocados
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Cilantro
  • Jalapeños
  • Lime juice
  • Garlic
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Mash avocados
  2. Add other Ingredients
  3. Mix them together
