Pumpkin Soup

Home

Description

This pumpkin soup recipe is creamy and healthy! It calls for roasted pumpkin for maximum flavor. This pumpkin soup is great with sandwiches and salads! Meet my favorite pumpkin soup recipe. It’s super creamy (thanks mostly to the pumpkin, with a little help from coconut milk or cream) yet plenty healthy, too. It’s gently spiced, but I made sure that the pumpkin flavor shines above the rest.

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin
  • Olive oil
  • Yellow onion
  • Heavy cream
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Slice up the pumpkin and blend it
  2. Heat olive oil
  3. Add blended pumpkin
  4. Mix and let rest for 8 minutes
  5. Add heavy cream and black pepper
